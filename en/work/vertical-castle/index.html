Vertical Castle Blockout

A multi-route castle blockout connecting cellars, rooftops and a city in the sky.

Watch on Bilibili (opens in a new tab)

THE PROJECT

Behind the scene.

A castle level blockout focused on vertical exploration. Its spatial organization references Stormveil Castle in Elden Ring, connecting underground cellars and a ground-level castle with an added multi-level sky castle.

Built from basic geometry in Unreal Engine 5, the level organizes courtyards, walls, rooftops and an aerial maze around four main routes to the summit. Elevators and ladders connect the different heights and guide players toward a destination approximately 130 meters above the base.
