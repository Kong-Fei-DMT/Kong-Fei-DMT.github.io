THE PROJECT

Behind the scene.

A Japanese castle interior built in Unreal Engine 5. Materials, lighting and shot composition create a narrative atmosphere while exploring environment production and technical integration.

Created by following Bad Decisions tutorials. The work covers scene assembly, modular windows, animated candle flames, cloth flags and character animation integration. Armor and clothing use external assets.
