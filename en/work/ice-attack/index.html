Magic Attack: Ice

A frost circle, an ice spear and erupting spikes shape the spell's charge and release.

Watch on Bilibili (opens in a new tab)

THE PROJECT

Behind the scene.

An ice-element attack effect built in Unreal Engine 5. The sequence moves from a charging magic circle to an ice spear launch and erupting spikes, emphasizing casting rhythm and impact.

The work includes magic-circle design, ice-material tuning, layered particles and timing control. Cold mist, falling light, character motion and camera shake complete the casting sequence.

Focus
Magic-circle design / Ice materials / Particles & timing
