Sci-Fi Hangar

A Star Wars-inspired hangar study in science-fiction lighting and cinematic camera work.

Preview hereWatch on Bilibili (opens in a new tab)

THE PROJECT

Behind the scene.

A Star Wars-inspired hangar scene created by following Bad Decisions tutorials. Unreal Engine 5 environment modeling and cross-application asset integration are combined with parameterized PBR materials, procedural textures and decals to build an industrial science-fiction setting.

Lumen real-time rendering and path tracing shape the lighting, while Nanite supports high-detail assets. Character and spacecraft animations are arranged in Sequencer; cinematic cameras and camera rails provide shot design for the final 4K image sequences.

Focus
PBR materials / Lumen & path tracing / Sequencer cinematography
